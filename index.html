Companion certificates let you bring a second traveler for only taxes & fees when you buy a qualifying ticket. Delta and American cards offer these annually. The key question: what's the companion ticket's cash value versus your effective certificate cost (card fee minus other benefits)?
Free night certificates come with hotel credit cards and cover a standard room — sometimes at any property worldwide (Hilton Aspire: Waldorf Astoria, Conrad) or within a category cap (Marriott, Hyatt). Enter what you'd pay cash and what the card costs you after other benefits to find the true value.
